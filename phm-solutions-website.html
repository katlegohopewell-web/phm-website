Sheet 01 — Available now

FlowIQ

Peak flow calculations across five SANRAL Drainage Manual methods — cross-checked against the manual's own worked examples, not just internally consistent.

  • 01Five methods in one place — Rational Method (both alternatives), Standard Design Flood, Unit Hydrograph, and the Empirical method — sharing your catchment data so you enter it once, not five times.
  • 02Design rainfall pulled automatically from the SA Weather Bureau's station archive, just by entering coordinates.
  • 03Every figure the manual references is one click away — the actual chart, not just a citation.
  • 04Project letterhead and one-click PDF export, for each method and for a side-by-side comparison summary.
  • 05Runs on your PC. No internet needed to calculate — it checks in periodically just to confirm your subscription.
